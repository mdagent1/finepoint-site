Working together

A copywriter can make or break how your brand shows up in the world.

And I believe that goes beyond creativity and craft; equally important is the working relationship and mutual understanding of the vision, goals, and operations behind your brand — and mine.

These packages are a focused, low-risk way for us to gauge how well we work together. You get a sense of what it’s like to work with me (how I write, think, and collaborate), and I get the chance to experience your brand, process, and business needs firsthand.

Packages

  • The Landing Page

    Starting at$250

    Writing or rewriting one core webpage (e.g., Home, About, Services), delivered polished and ready to go live. One round of revisions included.

  • The Email Series

    Starting at$315

    Writing three emails for a single campaign or initiative, each with two subject line and preview text options for A/B testing. Delivered polished and ready to deploy. One round of revisions included.

  • The Precision Edit

    Starting at$110

    Proofreading a single asset (up to 1,500 words) for spelling, grammar, punctuation, and brand-specific conventions. Delivered polished and ready to use.
