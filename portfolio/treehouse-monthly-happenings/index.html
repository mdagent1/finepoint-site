Starwood · Treehouse Hotels

Transforming monthly programming into an editorial experience.

Client
Starwood · Treehouse Hotels
Industry
Hospitality
Format
Editorial email series
Focus
Brand voice stewardship, Editorial storytelling, Guest engagement, Lifecycle marketing

The opportunity

Treehouse Hotel London’s monthly happenings emails were designed to extend the brand beyond the property for existing and prospective guests, transforming a calendar of events into an editorial experience that showcased upcoming dining experiences, rooftop programming, and seasonal celebrations. Each edition required fresh storytelling while preserving the brand’s unmistakably playful tone of voice.

The takeaway

A distinctive brand voice is one that is reinforced over time. For recurring content, success isn’t measured by a single edition; it’s earned by creating a recognizable voice that guests can return to month after month.
