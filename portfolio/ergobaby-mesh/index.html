Ergobaby

Reframing warm-weather babywearing around comfort and connection.

Client
Ergobaby
Industry
Consumer goods
Campaign
Email, paid ads, web banners
Focus
Cross-channel copywriting, Brand voice, Consumer marketing

The opportunity

Breathability was the product feature. Connection was the story. This seasonal campaign reframed mesh babywearing as more than a warm-weather solution, pairing practical product benefits with messaging that reflected the everyday moments parents value most.

The takeaway

Every campaign begins with the same question: Why should someone care? For me, the answer is almost always found first in the human experience, with the product playing a naturally supportive role within the broader story. Because features earn attention, but emotion earns connection. And the most effective campaigns find room for both.
