04On AI

No one asked, but since everyone’s asking, here is my position on AI.

Your brand always leads. While I am a big proponent of humans writing for humans, I understand that sometimes the primary goals are speed and volume.

I welcome AI-eager clientele with the caveat that I will always contribute time and effort to fact check, revise, and refine all AI-generated copy, ensuring that it meets not only the standards for your brand, but for mine as well.
